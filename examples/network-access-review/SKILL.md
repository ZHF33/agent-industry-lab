Follow README; submit synthetic request identifiers only. Inspect request, policy, owner and computed access_report. Literal scope matching is not access authorization or proof of safety. Preserve thread_id for human review. Never scan, connect to requested addresses, change firewall rules, issue credentials or send messages. Keep keys/traces local; do not start services or blindly retry ambiguous submissions.
